# The BCAT Manifesto

We live at a moment where machines can act,
but humans no longer feel certain they should let them.

Boundary-Conditioned Autonomy and Trust exists because
capability alone does not produce legitimacy.

BCAT asserts that:
- autonomy must be earned
- authority must be conditional
- trust must persist, not be assumed
- refusal can be a success
- humans must remain sovereign

We do not promise perfect alignment.
We do not promise complete foresight.
We do not claim to have solved the future.

We acknowledge that harmonious coexistence between humans and autonomous systems
may require ethical, social, and existential development beyond current knowledge.

BCAT is not an answer to all questions.

It is a commitment to never let systems act
without asking whether they still should.

We believe trust emerges not from power,
but from visible limits.

And we choose to build those limits deliberately.
