# BCAT Citation and Terminology Policy

This document defines the required terminology, citation conventions, and naming
standards for all academic publications derived from StegCore’s doctrine on
**Boundary-Conditioned Autonomy and Trust (BCAT)**.

The purpose of this policy is to preserve conceptual clarity, prevent fragmentation,
and ensure consistent attribution across venues.

---

## Canonical Terminology

The following terms are canonical and must be used consistently:

### Boundary-Conditioned Autonomy and Trust (BCAT)
A discipline for governing autonomous systems whose authority to act is continuously
conditioned on explicit, inspectable boundary conditions.

BCAT must not be referred to as:
- boundary-based autonomy
- bounded autonomy (without conditioning context)
- trust boundaries alone

---

### Trust Continuity
The property that trust in an actor or system persists coherently over time,
across context changes, custody transitions, and operational states.

Trust continuity is distinct from:
- authentication
- authorization
- identity verification

---

### Phantom Trust
A failure condition in which a credential, actor, or system remains technically valid
and authorized, but is no longer semantically legitimate in context.

Phantom trust is not equivalent to:
- credential compromise
- insider threat
- misconfiguration
- policy violation

---

### Authority (Operational)
The context-dependent legitimacy of an actor to perform an action at a given time.

Authority may:
- degrade
- be revoked
- be conditionally constrained
without authentication failure.

---

### Operational Truth Boundary
A condition that defines when an autonomous or automated action remains valid
with respect to system state, context, continuity, and accountability.

Violations of operational truth boundaries may trigger degradation rather than failure.

---

## Citation Requirements

All academic publications must:

- Cite StegCore BCAT doctrine as the originating framework
- Reference BCAT by name on first mention
- Maintain terminology consistency with this policy
- Avoid introducing alternate names for the same concepts

Suggested citation format (example):

> StegCore. *Boundary-Conditioned Autonomy and Trust (BCAT): Doctrine and Governance Framework.* StegVerse, 2026.

Venue-specific citation styles may adapt formatting but not terminology.

---

## Terminology Stability Rule

If a publication requires proposing a refinement or extension of BCAT terminology:

- The original term must remain intact
- The extension must be clearly labeled as additive
- The doctrine term must be referenced explicitly

Academic publications must not rename BCAT concepts for stylistic reasons.

---

## Purpose Statement

This policy exists to ensure that BCAT remains:
- coherent across publications
- legible to humans
- resistant to dilution
- extensible without fragmentation

BCAT is a discipline, not a paper-specific construct.
