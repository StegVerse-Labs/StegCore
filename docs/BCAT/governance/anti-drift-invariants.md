# Anti-Drift Invariants (StegCore Guardrails)

This document defines the invariants that prevent StegVerse from drifting
away from its core philosophy of **Boundary-Conditioned Autonomy and Trust (BCAT)**.

These invariants are non-negotiable.

They are designed to hold under:
- scale
- success
- automation pressure
- increasing AI capability
- incomplete human foresight

## Invariant 1 — Authority Is Never Implicit

No system, agent, workflow, or apparatus may assume authority by virtue of:
- capability
- longevity
- prior success
- convenience
- automation inheritance

Authority must be:
- explicitly evaluated
- contextually justified
- revocable without failure

## Invariant 2 — Capability Does Not Confer Legitimacy

Growth in system capability does not expand authority automatically.

Legitimacy must be re-evaluated as:
- context changes
- assumptions decay
- custody shifts
- time passes

Systems may become more capable while becoming *less legitimate*.

## Invariant 3 — Boundaries Must Be Inspectable

All boundary conditions governing autonomy must be:
- explicit
- inspectable
- auditable
- explainable to humans

Implicit boundaries are considered nonexistent.

## Invariant 4 — Non-Action Is a Valid Outcome

Refusal, degradation, or suspension of action due to boundary conditions
is considered a correct and successful system behavior.

Any design that treats refusal as failure violates BCAT.

## Invariant 5 — Human Sovereignty Is Preserved

Automation does not dissolve responsibility.

Every autonomous action must remain traceable to:
- accountable human custody
- explainable authority decisions
- revocable governance paths

No system may obscure responsibility behind abstraction.

## Invariant 6 — Drift Signals Are First-Class

Signals of philosophical or operational drift are not noise.

They include:
- silent escalation of authority
- bypass mechanisms without doctrine reference
- “temporary” overrides without sunset
- automation that outlives its assumptions

These signals must trigger review, not normalization.

---

## Statement of Incompleteness

These invariants do not claim to solve all ethical, existential,
or societal challenges of autonomy.

They assert only this:
> **Autonomous systems must remain governable even when human understanding is incomplete.**

BCAT is designed to hold precisely because future conditions are unknown.
