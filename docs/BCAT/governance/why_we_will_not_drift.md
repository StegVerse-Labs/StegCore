# Why StegVerse Will Not Drift

Most systems drift because their values are advisory.

StegVerse does not treat values as advice.
It treats them as conditions of legitimacy.

BCAT is not an aspiration.
It is a gate.

As StegVerse evolves, we expect:
- new capabilities
- stronger AI
- unfamiliar behaviors
- pressures we cannot yet anticipate

We explicitly acknowledge that harmonious coexistence between humans and
autonomous systems may require forms of ethical, social, and existential
development that are unknown at the time of this writing.

BCAT does not attempt to predict those futures.

Instead, it ensures that:
- authority remains conditional
- trust remains inspectable
- humans retain sovereignty
- systems can refuse themselves

We will not drift because:
- no component can outrun StegCore
- no success grants unchecked authority
- no automation escapes explanation
- no future overrides accountability

StegVerse is designed to evolve.
It is not designed to forget why it exists.
