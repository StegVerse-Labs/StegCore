## Formalism now preserved

The v5 review records:

1. **Action First** — ALLOW is a possibility/admissibility determination, not execution and not continuity.
2. **Admissibility cloud** — multiple pre-transition candidates may be admitted before reality contact.
3. **Irreversibility boundary** — one modeled irreversible event becomes one realized successor transition; undo requires a new compensating transition.
4. **Immediate cloud regeneration** — every realized successor state immediately has a new admissibility field.
5. **Calculated / realized / observed state split** — `S_hat`, `S_real`, and `S_obs` are distinct unless convergence is demonstrated.
6. **Observability as state identity** — a state's observable identity may change even when its underlying ontic state does not.
7. **Reality collapse vs observation collapse** — an event may be real before it is recorded or reconstructable.
8. **Dual delta model** — model-to-reality residual `Δ_R` is distinct from reality-to-observation residual `Δ_O`.
9. **Full admissibility-matrix preservation** — counterfactual possibility must survive reconstruction without being mistaken for unrealized history.
10. **Effective constraints** — known, unknown, latent/unknown-unknown, and irreducible uncertainty are separated.
11. **Constraint discovery** — repeated identifiable residual structure may promote latent structure toward known governance constraints.
12. **Reconstruction efficiency** — prediction fit alone is insufficient; causal reconstructability and observation coverage remain separate dimensions.
13. **Viability** — continuity of a continuing system includes capacity for future admissible transitions; intentional terminal states remain valid.
14. **Reconstruction Singularity** — asymptotic convergence of unexplained transition error and latent constraint uncertainty toward irreducible observational/physical uncertainty, never model=reality.
