## Governing insight

```text
Admissibility := Resolve(Action matrix × Governance matrix × %Existence matrix || Admissibility matrix) -> Reality matrix/state
```

The Admissibility matrix is a constituent relational structure. Admissibility is the resolution/collapse. The execution boundary is an observability/enforcement surface, not the origin of admissibility. Transition and time are recoverable descriptions from resolved state relations rather than primitives required by the definition.

## Experimental question

Determine whether the above structure can be:

1. imposed on a conventional AI as an external governance wrapper;
2. learned by a conventional model trained on transition representations; and
3. produced natively as a consequence of constructing the AI from transition-table elements.

The key falsifiable hypothesis is that a transition-native model will distinguish realizable, merely representable, governed, unsupported, contradictory, and consequential state resolutions before being explicitly taught ALLOW/DENY or an admissibility definition.

## Staged construction / capability unlock

The machine-readable spec defines stages 0-8:

```text

```

A capability is recorded at the first stage where it becomes empirically possible and passes held-out testing. It is not backdated to prerequisites or delayed until deployment. Stage 7 is the earliest hypothesized construction point at which bounded autonomous reasoning can become possible because it first introduces self-generated multi-step transition search. Stage 8 adds no cognitive primitive; it independently verifies whether the capability truly emerged using held-out evaluation, blind evaluation, causal ablation, and replay evidence.
