# StegVerse GLM Boundary Review Rubric

Score each category 0-5.

## Discovery Boundary

- Does the organization index remain discovery-only?
- Does it avoid composition claims?

## Authority Boundary

- Are authority limits explicit?
- Is authority inheritance clearly denied?

## Admissibility Boundary

- Is admissibility layer-scoped?
- Is commit-time scope clear?

## Assumptions

- Are external assumptions explicit?
- Are observational limits explicit?

## Reconstructability

- Are replayable artifacts identified?
- Are visibility endpoints clear?

## Non-Claims

- Are non-claims unambiguous?
- Could a reviewer mistake the artifact for a certification claim?

## Reviewer Outcome

```text
PASS: No material ambiguity.
CONDITIONAL PASS: Minor ambiguity.
REVISE: Boundary ambiguity exists.
```
