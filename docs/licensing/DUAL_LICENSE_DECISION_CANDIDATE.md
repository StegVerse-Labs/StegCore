**Commercial lane:** Separately negotiated agreement for commercial support, indemnities where agreed, proprietary redistribution/embedding where rights permit, enterprise SLAs and official conformance certification. No assumption that a commercial license overrides third-party grants or copyleft obligations.

**Marks:** StegVerse, StegCore and StegCore Verified marks are reserved for separate trademark/certification terms; possession of source-code rights never confers official governance authority or certified status.

**Governance:** Community recipients may study/modify code subject to the selected license. Claims of official StegVerse conformance require independently verified canonical runtime semantics, versioned conformance tests, current authority, and trademark permission. Do not impose field-of-use restrictions on an OSI-licensed code grant; use certification/mark rules and separate contracts instead.
